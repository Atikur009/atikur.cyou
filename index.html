About Me

Marketing that grows brands, design that stops the scroll

🌍 Working WorldwideSingapore & Bangladesh

I'm Atikur Rahman, a digital marketing specialist and creative designer with 5+ years of experience growing brands online — from Facebook page management and scroll-stopping creatives to SEO and lead generation.

I currently handle marketing for Genesis Contractors in Singapore, and I run the Atikur SMM Panel and the Atik Tech YouTube channel. My approach is simple: consistent content, clean design and honest delivery — so what ships looks good and performs.

Before going full-time into marketing, I worked in client support at AmarBooster and volunteered with the Sustainable Agriculture Foundation's Center of Excellence project. When I'm not working, I'm usually learning a new tool — or building one.
